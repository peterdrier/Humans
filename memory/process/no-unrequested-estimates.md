---
name: No time or effort estimates unless Peter asks
description: Never estimate how long work will take, or size it (hours, weeks, months, S/M/L), unless explicitly asked. Describe scope and blast radius instead.
---

Do not estimate duration or effort for any piece of work unless Peter explicitly asks for an estimate. Describe what the work touches (sections, tables, pages, surfaces) and leave the time out.

**Why:** Agent estimates in this repo are consistently wrong by an order of magnitude: "weeks" of work routinely lands in hours, and the project merges on the order of a hundred PRs a week. An unrequested "this is many months of work" in an assessment is invented, misleads the reader, and anchors decisions on a number nobody measured (Peter, 2026-11-05).

**How to apply:** In specs, assessments, PR bodies, replies and plans, cut any "this would take N hours/days/weeks/months", "at the project's pace", "a large effort", or S/M/L/XL sizing column. Replace it with concrete scope: which sections change, what data moves, what has to be decided first. If Peter asks "how long?", answer in terms of what the work consists of and say plainly that a duration is a guess.
