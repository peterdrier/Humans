## Findings

1. **Two Active seasons, unordered pick.** After an auto-approved renewal a camp holds an Active
   season in two years. `CampController.Members` builds its roles panel from the first Active/Full
   season, and `CampService.AddCampMemberToActiveSeasonAsync` and
   `AddMemberAndAssignRoleInActiveSeasonAsync` pick the first Active/Full season with no year filter;
   `CampRepository.GetByIdAsync` loads seasons unordered, so these can target last year. Which season
   should win (public year, the page's year, latest) is a product call. Tests thread corroborates: no
   test pins the choice.
2. **Dead uncached compliance path** (fixed). The decorator serves `ICampRoleCampAccess` and computes the
   compliance list from its cache; the inner compliance-season method, the repository's
   all-camps-for-year read and a public-season predicate on `Camp` were unreachable, and a keyed
   registration existed only to forward member-status lookups. Reviewer approved on resubmission.

   implements it itself; the inner copy was never called.

   listed Draft/Submitted states, a request-changes path and approval notifications that do not exist.
5. **Season open/close and name-lock writes are unaudited** (ledgered as CENTRAL-82). `AuditAction` has
   no member for them, so the fix spans AuditLog.
6. **Hardcoded English lead banner** on the public camp page, plus English breadcrumb labels on the lead
   Edit and Members pages (fixed, reusing the existing breadcrumb key).
7. **False comments** (fixed): comments named a refresh method, a leave method and a compliance-report

8. **Camps.md drift** (fixed): public-year claim, CampAdmin capabilities row, an `IsLead` flag that does
