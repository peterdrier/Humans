## Assessment summary

First doctor run on Camps. The section is structurally sound — one aggregate
repository plus a role lane, a single caching decorator owning the `CampInfo`
projection, thin slug-keyed controllers — and its hard parts (consumed-EE
retention, account-merge fold, role-backed lead authority) are both implemented
and pinned. What it carries is sediment from two migrations (the section
carve-out and the Camp Lead retirement, the latter narrated six times) plus a
real seam the target shape exposed: lead-facing mutations that take a raw
form id without proving it belongs to the resolved camp (M1), and a Rejoin
authority mismatch between code and every doc that describes it (M2). Both are
privilege questions and went to the queue untouched. Target shape:
`src/Sections/Humans.Camps/Docs/health.md` (new this run).
