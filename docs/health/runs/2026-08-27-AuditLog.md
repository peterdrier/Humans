`IAuditLogRepository.GetRecentAsync` (each with a single upstream caller in the chain), so the
whole chain plus its 4 pinning tests and an orphaned test stub came out. Reviewer-gated
(score-blind, default-reject); approved.

Findings 2–4 are the doc drift the deletion surfaced, plus one contract-comment accuracy fix
found by reading the query against its own doc.
