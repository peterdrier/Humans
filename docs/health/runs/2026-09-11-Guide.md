## Assessment summary

Guide is the in-app volunteer handbook: it fetches `docs/guide/*.md` from GitHub, wraps each
`## As a …` block in a role div, renders it through Markdig, rewrites its links, caches the HTML,
and strips blocks by role per request. It owns no tables and no resource set.

The target shape was re-derived from scratch — the previous run left a scorecard, not a claim
about what is — and the section matched it structurally. What did not match was its prose: the
docs and comments carried two deleted project names, an invented pinning test, a consumer count
that contradicted its own list in the same sentence, a file-count claim that had been wrong since
the corpus grew, and one negative access rule that a passing test refutes. The one behaviour
defect (finding 1) came from running the shipped corpus through the real pipeline rather than from
any scan.

Reforge, this run, on the section: `Guide 26 loc=943 cogP95=13 cogMax=20`
(`GuideMarkdownPreprocessor.Wrap`), `maxClassLoc=137` (`GuideHtmlPostprocessor`). The published
figure is the PR's surface report, recomputed against the head that shipped.

Independence check: pass. Findings 2, 12 and 16 came from the target rather than from a scan —
2 is a spec-vs-reality delta §4.5 exposed (no tool flags a doc contradicting a passing test), 12 is
a §1 claim about how a reader gets back out that no detector measures, and 16 is a shape mismatch
against §3's "each step of the pipeline has exactly one home".
