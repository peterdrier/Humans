## Assessment summary

First doctor of Pleo. The section is a read-only mirror of the Pleo card wallet with four finance-admin pages and a two-method contract Finance books from. Its code is sound and well guarded at the vendor boundary: a short or malformed read can never shrink the mirror, and a failed category fetch leaves categories as they were. The drift was in what its docs and comments say: the data-access doc denied that sweeps write categories, the dependency list named a project the section does not reference, the architecture section described a refactor instead of the code, and two comments still said category ids are only linked by hand. Walking the shapes found two behaviours worth Peter's ruling: a hand unlink is undone by the next sweep, and the blocking counts on `/Pleo` count less than the lists they link to.

Independence check: pass. Findings 1 and 2 came from walking the target's shapes (S4 and S2), not from a tool or a thread.

## Findings

1. A hand unlink of a category is undone by the next sweep that reads Pleo's categories: the unlinked row keeps its name, so `PlanCategories` matches it by name and relinks the same id with `SetByUserId` cleared. Latent while the key lacks category read access.
2. The two blocking counts on `/Pleo` count completed spend only, but the filtered lists they link to (`?unmapped=true`, `?noReceipt=true`) also show pending, unclassified and no-EUR entries, so a list can be longer than its count.
3. `Docs/data-access.md` said `pleo_categories` is never written by a sweep, and named `Service` as the only repository caller.
4. `Docs/Pleo.md` listed `Users.Contracts` as a dependency; `IUserService` and `HumansControllerBase` live in Base and the project references only Base and `Finance.Contracts`.
5. `Docs/Pleo.md` Architecture narrated a refactor ("unchanged", "no DI registration is added"), with the sweep paragraph misplaced under Public contract.
6. `Docs/Pleo.md` cited a Pleo connector spec that does not exist.
7. The first two Invariants bullets in `Docs/Pleo.md` were implementation-level run-on lines (UTF-16 units, "existing refusal").
8. The `/Pleo` sync card said a sweep makes three to five calls (it reads five endpoints, four of them paged) and that it runs at 03:15 without saying UTC; `Docs/Pleo.md` Triggers had the same time without zone.
9. Comments: `PleoEntry.CategoryId` said the category-to-account API is closed to the key, the seed comment said ids are linked by hand, and `Section.cs` and `PleoEmployee` understated what a cardholder row holds.
10. No test pinned the one-sweep-at-a-time skip, the hand unlink and add audit entries, or the duplicate-name refusal.
11. The entries list and entry page show the original-currency amount as minors over a hundred, which assumes two decimals.
12. Several tests are theories with permutation rows (cancellation boundaries, incomplete money, text bounds, non-object bodies).
13. peterdrier/Humans#2430's file:line references have drifted; the issue still stands.
14. Conformance detector: `PleoAuditActions.cs` sits at the project root outside the allowed set.
15. Two comments in `Humans.Pleo.csproj` restate the line under them.
