## Tickets Glossary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Human** | A member of Nobodies Collective. We say "humans", not "members" or "volunteers". |
| **Order** | Everything one buyer pays for at once, settled as a single payment: one or more tickets, plus donations. |
| **Ticket** | One person's entry to one event: the code the gate scans. Every ticket names its holder. |
| **Buyer** | The human who pays for an order. |
| **Holder** | The person whose legal name is on a ticket. |
| **Partner** | A holder on an order who is not its buyer, usually a friend of the buyer. |
| **VIP ticket** | A ticket at the current price plus a large donation, at a fixed total. It carries no privileges. |
| **Discount** | A fixed amount off a ticket's price, from a discount program the human qualifies for. |
| **Resale** | A holder who cannot come puts their ticket up for someone else to buy. They are refunded once it sells. |
| **Ticketing platform** | The system that takes payment for an event's orders at the price Humans sets: Box Office, pretix or Hi.Events. |
| **Ticketing settings** | One event's ticketing configuration: its ticketing platform, sales mode, waves, prices and switches. |
| **Ticket Vendor** | TicketTailor, which sold tickets up to 2026. Its orders are synced into Humans and kept as history. |
| **Sync** | The process of pulling TicketTailor's ticket data into the system. |
| **Discount Code** | A TicketTailor code that took money off an order, minted for a campaign. |
| **TicketsAdmin** | A role with access to ticket management, sync operations, and discount codes. |
