## Tickets

The tickets section sells event tickets through each event's ticketing platform, and keeps the orders synced in from
TicketTailor, the ticket vendor up to 2026.

### Key Functions

- **Buy tickets** — buy a ticket for yourself, your partners and your children, at your own price
- **My tickets** — see your tickets with their codes, put one up for resale, or ask for a companion ticket
- **Ticketing settings** — set each event's ticketing platform, sales mode, waves, prices and switches
- **Orders** — find any order, see its timeline, refund it, rename or void a ticket
- **View TicketTailor orders** — browse the ticket orders synced from the vendor
- **Sync operations** — trigger manual sync or view sync history
- **Discount codes** — manage promotional codes for ticket sales

### What Happens Automatically

- Order status is read again from the ticketing platform after payment and every few minutes until it settles
- Tickets and receipts are emailed once an order is paid
- Resale refunds go out in an evening batch once the ticket has sold
- Ticket orders are checked against each ticketing platform every night, and the ticketing team is told what does not balance
- Ticket data syncs from TicketTailor on a regular schedule
